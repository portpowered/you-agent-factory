## Codebase Patterns
- Published Factory Session fixture scenario IDs and stable projection hashes live in `pkg/factorysessionexecution/fixture_catalog.go` (`PublishedFixtureScenarios`, `FixtureScenarioIdentityFromScenario`, `FixtureScenarioProjectionHash`); contract JSON remains in `pkg/api/testdata/durable-session-contract-fixtures.json` and loads through `LoadFakeScenariosFromContractFixtures`.
- Use `ContractFixtureCatalogRelativePath` when downstream cells need the repository-relative catalog path; prove stable identity with `fixture_catalog_test.go` golden projection hashes rather than recomputing ad hoc in each consumer package.
- Built-in recoverable interrupted JavaScript session `javascript-interrupted-recoverable` is appended by `LoadFakeScenariosFromContractFixtures` even though it is not in the JSON catalog.
