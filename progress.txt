## Codebase Patterns
- Deterministic fake durable sessions live in `pkg/factorysessionexecution/` (`FakeService`, `NewFakeServiceFromContractFixtures`, `WithFakeScenarios`); scenarios are keyed by execution `requestId` and loaded from `pkg/api/testdata/durable-session-contract-fixtures.json` plus built-in `BuiltinInterruptedRecoverableScenario()`.
- Prove fake projections at the service layer in `pkg/factorysessionexecution/fake_service_test.go` and through apisurface mappers in `pkg/apisurface/factorysession/factory_session_fake_consumer_test.go`.
- Start idempotency replays on `requestId` via `startReplay` map; conflicting tuples return `ErrExecutionRequestIDConflict`. Concurrent starts are safe under `FakeService.mu`.
- Fixture `asyncResponse.links` may be partial; stability on replay matters more than requiring every inspection link field to be populated.
- Use `workflowsource.KindFactoryID` (not string literals) when constructing `factorysessionexecution.StartRequest` in tests.
- Fixture `session` rows may omit `progress`/`artifactCount` while `listSummary` carries them; call `enrichSessionReadProjection` when loading scenarios so `GetSession` detail matches scoped list rows and persisted seeds.
- Use `ValidateSessionDetailMatchesListSummary` to prove list/detail coherence for phase, progress, result summary, artifact count, actions, links, and recoverability.
- Final result mode returns `NOT_READY` for running and interrupted sessions that only have partial progress; `FAILED_WITH_PARTIAL` remains the terminal final read for failed-with-partial scenarios.
- Use `ValidateDispatchDetailMatchesListSummary` and `ValidateArtifactDetailMatchesListSummary` to prove dispatch/artifact list/detail coherence; artifact detail may carry retrieval refs on `ContentRef` while list rows use `RetrievalRef`.
- `enrichArtifactDetailFromListSummary` copies list retrieval refs onto sparse artifact detail fixtures during scenario load.
- Use `ValidateEventReplayMatchesDirectProjections` to prove ordered fake event streams align with direct session, result, dispatch, and artifact reads; `FilterEventsAfterReconnect` and service `ReadEvents` honor `AfterEventID`/`AfterSequence` cursors and return `ErrReconnectCursorNotFound` for unknown cursors.
- Scenarios without fixture `events` derive canonical `SESSION_STARTED`/`SESSION_RESULT_UPDATED`/`SESSION_COMPLETED` streams via `BuildCanonicalSessionEvents` on scenario load.
