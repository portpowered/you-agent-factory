## Codebase Patterns
- Document `pkg/services/events` as the process-local, in-memory, session-scoped source-native observation stream; Recordings owns canonical Factory Event JSONL history, replay, artifacts, and historical projections.
- Architecture service inventories should name the package that publishes the public owner contract, and diagrams should show direct service operations unless a bounded delivery contract is explicitly involved.
- Define ACP customer terms from the detached `chat_sessions` and `worker_sessions` public contracts; use customer-facing relationship diagrams and keep their event lifetimes separate from Recordings history.
- Event documentation has two shipped contracts only: `workers.Kind`/`workers.Phase` observations retain source provenance, while Recordings `FactoryEvent` JSONL artifacts supply canonical replay history.
