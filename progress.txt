## Codebase Patterns
- ACP outbound mappers are pure and must preserve Chat Sessions' stored ItemID and ParentItemID rather than allocating transport-local identities.
- Factory child ownership must be established by the canonical dispatch-to-Worker-Session association; malformed or unaddressed child records fail closed without an ACP fallback.
- A live event reader may use a cancellable context, but committed sequencing and terminal catch-up must use a non-cancelled delivery context so a record observed at the invocation boundary is not lost.
- When a live source is replayed from its retained origin during terminal catch-up, retain successfully committed source identities for the current bridge run before applying lifecycle-order guards.
- Only an associated SESSION/STARTED record opens a Factory child tool call; every other associated Worker record must preserve the sequencer-assigned opening ItemID as ParentItemID, even when its phase is STARTED.
- Isolate typed mapping failures only for associated child records at the delivery boundary; preserve the established fatal behavior for malformed generic Chat envelopes.
